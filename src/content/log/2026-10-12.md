Last note of the TypeScript series today. Thirty wrong-vs-right cards, and
it took longer than the other fifteen notes combined because every card
needed a code example that actually compiled.

Looked back at the sixteen notes afterwards. The ones that stuck are the
ones where I had to argue with myself — interface vs type, narrowing,
discriminated unions. The ones I do not remember are the ones that were
only definitions.

That is a useful thing to learn about how I learn. A note I did not have to
argue with was a note I did not need.
