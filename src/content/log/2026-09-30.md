The null/undefined note took longer than it should because I kept arguing
with myself about whether `?` should mean "may be missing" or "may be
undefined".

Landed on: the optional marker describes the *caller*, not the value. A
property that can be missing needs a different check than a property that
can be undefined. They are not the same thing and the compiler treats them
differently.

Kept the distinction and wrote the note.
