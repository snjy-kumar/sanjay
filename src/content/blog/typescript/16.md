What it is
</h2>
<p>This note closes the TypeScript series. The pages before it are the habits I kept after writing them down: precise basics, a rule for <code>interface</code> and <code>type</code>, unions, an honest story for absence, tuples when position matters, signatures on helpers, and <code>unknown</code> instead of <code>any</code>.</p>
<p>The later notes are the same idea with sharper tools. Narrowing is how a check becomes a type. A small generic preserves a caller’s type. <code>Pick</code>, <code>Omit</code>, and <code>Partial</code> stop me copying a model. Discriminated unions keep impossible UI state unrepresentable. Parsers reconnect JSON to those types. Mapped types plus <code>satisfies</code> keep a closed set of keys in one place. One real id bug was enough to make the boundary habit stick.</p>
<p>The scheduled title of this close mentions a card set. What I actually published here is one long note per habit, then this page. I am not going to pretend there is a second hidden pile of examples. The useful review is the list above.</p>
<p>If I open an older personal repo, I still find <code>as</code> and a wide <code>string</code> status. The close is not that those files are finished. It is that I know which lie to remove first, and I can point at a note that shows the before and after.</p>
</section>

<h2>

When to use which
</h2>
<p>When I come back to this series I reopen the note that matches the bug in front of me, not the note that sounds the most advanced. A wide status string sends me to unions. A crash on <code>null</code> sends me to narrowing. A copied model sends me to <code>Pick</code>. JSON sends me to <code>unknown</code>.</p>
<div class="when">
<div class="when-row"><span>A field with a few real states</span><span class="when-choice">unions or a discriminated union</span></div>
<div class="when-row"><span>A crash on a wide value</span><span class="when-choice">narrow, or parse unknown</span></div>
<div class="when-row"><span>A copied model or a flag map</span><span class="when-choice">Pick, Omit, Partial, or a mapped type</span></div>
<div class="when-row"><span>A helper that erased its result</span><span class="when-choice">a small generic</span></div>
</div>
</section>
